Logo

The logo.

Our logo is one simple lowercase word. There is no separate icon or symbol version. Only we decide how small details, like the dot on the "i" or the loop on the "e", look.

Choose a color

Minimum clear space

Leave empty space around the logo on every side. This space should be as tall as the lowercase "n" in the logo. Do not put text, images, or other logos inside that space.

Usage

Do / don't.

The logo works best when it stays exactly as it is. Here are the only changes you can make, and the mistakes to avoid.

Do

Use the SVG file at any size. It will always stay sharp and clear.

Don't

Do not stretch or squash the logo to fit a space. Always resize it evenly.

Do

Use the light version of the logo on dark, plain backgrounds.

Don't

Do not rotate or tilt the logo. It should always sit straight.

Do

Change the logo to one approved brand color only.

Don't

Do not use a color, gradient, or outline that is not one of our brand colors.

Do

Always leave enough empty space around the logo, as shown above.

Don't

Do not place the logo on a busy photo or a background that makes it hard to see.
